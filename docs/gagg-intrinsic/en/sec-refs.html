Statement on the use of AI

The setup of the calculations, the code of the Geant4 application and of the processing scripts, the literature search, and the text of this page were prepared with the assistance of AI. The following were used: Claude (Anthropic) — setup and orchestration of the calculations, writing and editing of code, literature search and reading, drafting and editing of text; the local models qwen3-coder:30b and qwen3.6:27b (Ollama) — generation of part of the code from a specification, a draft translation into English (later replaced by a translation by Claude), a back-translation for checking the meaning, and preliminary checks of the text.

What this means for the numbers. The numbers on the page were obtained by Geant4 calculations and scripts and exported from result files; they were not produced by text generation. The literature values (kB, activities, composition) were checked against the text of the sources: Furuno et al. 2021 — against the PDF on arXiv, Espinoza et al. 2024 — against the PDF, Belli et al. 2026 — against a copy of the text of the article; in this check, the work of Wang (CHEF 2019) served as the nominal density, without verification of the primary source.

What this does not guarantee. AI can make mistakes, and some of the errors were found only by independent checks (other AI instances and recalculation scripts; a local model as an evaluator has not been validated and is used only as a hint). Some statements remain not fully proven — see “Limitations and caveats.” The English version is a translation by Claude, checked by independent passes (a script check of the numbers, a back-translation by a local model with a check of the meaning, and a separate language review by other AI instances); it has not been proofread by a native speaker of English. A human, the author of the project, defined the tasks, chose the assumptions, and accepted the results; comments and reports of errors are accepted through the project repository (link below).
