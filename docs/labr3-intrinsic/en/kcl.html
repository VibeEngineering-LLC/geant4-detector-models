Intrinsic background of the LaBr3(Ce) crystal: template decomposition of the "Electrode and KCl" spectrum

The spectrum "Electrode and KCl" (live time 400 024 s, 8 805 293 counts) is compared with the background embedded in the same file (house background of 2026, same amplifier and scale; the operator's statement does not say whether this background was recorded inside the lead shield). A K-40 Gaussian is fitted on the "sample − background" difference; the Geant4 templates of the crystal's intrinsic radioactivity (instrument model v8, La-138 and the Ac-227 chain) describe the background as on the house-background page. The energy scale is taken from the file itself (smp.coef).

Result for the La-138 activity: first-principles expectation 7.62 Bq (range 7.27–7.83); fit 6.693 ± 0.012 (2025 background) and 6.677 ± 0.011 (2026 background) decays/s, ratio 0.878 and 0.876. An independent recalculation of the expectation (audit\sterile-act-2026-10-12.md) confirmed it. The 12 % deficit is not explained (see Assumptions).

Caveat on K-40: the K-40 line contains the intrinsic LaBr3 structure (the La-138 sum peak, almost entirely removed by the sample − embedded background difference) and the electrode's thorium. The Ac-228 line 1459.131 keV (yield 0.89 (6) % per decay, LNHB/CEA Ac-228 table, gamma-library) lies 1.7 keV from K-40 1460.851 keV (yield 10.34 (7) %, LNHB/CEA K-40 table, X. Mougeot), which is 14 times smaller than the peak FWHM, so LaBr3 does not resolve them. The Ac-228 contribution estimated from the 911.2 keV line (yield 26.5 (8) %): the net line area on the difference is 0.172 s⁻¹ (two-Gaussian fit, χ²/ndf 3.8), which gives at most 0.0058 s⁻¹ for 1459.1 keV without an efficiency correction, that is about 13 % of the K-40 area found (0.0445 s⁻¹ in the 1360–1560 keV window). The K-40 area depends on the fit window (0.0261 s⁻¹ in the 1380–1540 keV window); this uncertainty exceeds the statistical one. The line centroid on the difference is 1459.3 ± 0.3 keV (file scale), FWHM about 30 keV against 28.1 keV from the FWHM model at 1466 keV: the peak is wider than a single line, and a superposition of electrode Th-chain lines and the La-138 sum peak is not excluded. Detection efficiency at 911 and 1459 keV was not calculated, so the K-40 activity cannot be determined from these numbers.

Assumptions and what was not verified

  • No nonproportionality: electron and gamma light is taken proportional to the deposited energy, the Ce activator was not modelled. This is the main cause of residuals below 100 keV and near the edges.
  • The La-138 β⁻ shape in Geant4 (per the source code: a "classical" sampler on a 101-node grid, piecewise-constant density) is assumed to coincide with the classical shape, and the empirical correction C_exp(W) (Quarati, Sandler) is applied as a weight. It was not verified independently: the downloaded Mougeot papers (2014, 2015) give no closed expressions for F0, L0, λ2(W), λ3(W); a Dirac-equation solver and the Behrens–Bühring book were not used. The sampler-grid correction worsened χ²/ndf by 0.061.
  • The cause of the χ²/ndf shift of the v8 template relative to v7 (a decrease by 0.093 in 2025 and by 0.063 in 2026, χ²/ndf 2.39 and 2.44) is not established: the shift comes entirely from the La-138 template (0.093), the Ac-227 template gives 0.000123. It may be the two FR4 bodies or the statistics of the new template (other seeds); the two can be separated only by a v7 rerun with other seeds, which was not done.
  • The Pb Kβ/Kα area ratio in the fit is 0.45 (2025) and 0.44 (2026) against 0.27 expected from the handbook's relative intensities: the discrepancy is not explained.
  • Lead is a fact of the measurement; it was not put into the instrument model, the Pb lines are Gaussians with the reference FWHM. Below 100 keV (37 keV peak, instrument threshold) the model does not describe the data, so the main fit starts at 100 keV.
  • THE La-138 ACTIVITY DEFICIT IS NOT EXPLAINED: the fit gives 6.693 ± 0.012 and 6.677 ± 0.011 decays/s against the expected 7.62 Bq (range 7.27–7.83), ratio 0.878 and 0.876, deficit 12 % and 12 %. The expectation was recalculated independently (audit\sterile-act-2026-10-12.md) and confirmed. The size and density of the real instrument's crystal were not checked (taken from the model); the density 5.08 g/cm³ was found on a supplier page, not in a manufacturer's document.
  • The independent fit (6.303 ± 0.0069 decays/s) differs from the main one by 3.1–6.2 %; its χ²/ndf 48.9 is explained by the data binning (0.37 keV channels were put into 1 keV bins without redistribution, 2 and 3 channels in neighbouring bins), not by the component set.
  • The K-40 column in the model is zero; the only K-40 estimate is the area A3 in the doublet fit (534.3 ± 7 497 in 2025, at the bound in 2026), indistinguishable from zero. The 2026 double ratio has formal errors: A3 sits at the bound.
  • The source is decays inside the crystal only; the surroundings (housing, reflector, board) come from the GDML model v8; external gamma lines other than Pb were not put into the fit; the external continuum is described by smooth shapes.
  • Geant4 version 11.2.1 (the project's GDML build); it has a known instability of option4 with PIXE, run chunks were restarted; there were no restarts in the v8 run. The origin of the 14 events with an extra cluster is not established; the rate on v8 equals the rate on v7 within counting statistics (13 and 14 per 2·10⁷).
  • Alpha quenching is an external coefficient q (a common factor on the energy axis of the alpha template) chosen by the fit; its energy dependence is not modelled. Absolute detection efficiency was not used.
  • FWHM(E) is a model from the Nano 5 reference points (fwhm_points_nano5.json); the peak shape and resolution are not recomputed for each spectrum (#DET-1).
  • Reproducibility of chunks by seed and the bias of restarts were not checked; the geometry of v8 was compared with v7 only by the list of bodies and the summary.
