**Absorbed dose, μGy/h**

Absorbed dose is the energy deposited in tissue, without a radiation-type weighting (1 μGy/h = 0.001 mGy/h).

**Equivalent dose (wR per ICRP 103), μSv/h**

Equivalent dose is the absorbed dose multiplied by the wR factor (ICRP 103): for neutrons and protons it exceeds 1, so their share of the equivalent dose is larger than their share of the absorbed dose.
