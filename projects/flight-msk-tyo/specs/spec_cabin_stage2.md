Stage II of a two-stage cosmic-ray simulation: particles recorded in stage I at the side surface of a 14 cm radius tube in an aircraft cabin are replayed onto a scintillation spectrometer (CsI crystal). The energy deposited in the crystal is histogrammed with statistical weights.

`T_sim_s` = total simulated flight time represented by ALL the given psp files (sum of the `T_sim_s` of their `.meta` files); `T_flight_s` = flight duration used for the delayed-decay weight; `kB_cm_per_MeV` = Birks constant for heavy charged particles (0 = no quenching). ## Constants
`kEll_cm = 30.0` (window), `kL_cm = 300.0` (tube length), `kTubeR_mm = 139.0` (injection radius, 1 mm inside the 140 mm half width of the world), `kTrigNs = 1000.0` (resolving time), `kNBin = 10000` (1 keV bins, 0..10000 keV; one extra overflow bin at index kNBin).

## Mapping cabin -> device frame (exact)
For a record `r` of the group and the group centre `zd` returned by ChooseGroup: `xr = kTubeR_mm * cos(phi)`, `zr = kTubeR_mm * sin(phi)` (cabin x, z relative to the tube axis), `yr = (r.y_cm - zd) * 10` (mm). Device position `(X, Y, Z) = (xr, zr, yr)` mm. Device direction `(dX, dY, dZ) = (ux, uz, uy)`. The primary vertex time is `r.t_ns * ns`.
